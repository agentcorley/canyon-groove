How it works: A canyon cross-section is sampled as an elevation profile, west wall to east wall. Elevation maps to oscillator frequency (lower terrain = lower pitch), mirroring how vinyl groove depth modulates a stylus. The west wall drives the left channel; the east wall drives the right. The canyon floor is the groove bottom. Reverb decay time simulates acoustic resonance inside the canyon walls. Each canyon becomes a unique “record” — its geology encoded as sound.
